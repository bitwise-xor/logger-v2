Decisions taken without asking (neutral defaults):
- Undo has no second confirmation: pressing the button is the yes.
- In a Todoist task's edit form, an emptied date field means "unchanged"; removing a date needs the tick box.
- A signed-out stranger gets a plain 404 for an unknown page (no redirect), so no page name is revealed.
- A click on a done square removes all of that day's ticks, not just one.
- Past-day ticks and Wake-up: Wake-up is read-only in the calendar because a date-only wake time would corrupt sleep.
- New habits get the name's lower-case letters and digits as their key (`yoga_class`), kind event, category "daily", and go last.
- Trends and Week count only complete days (today left out), like the Sheet.
- The Week page's "how far from the target" is the average day as a share of the target (the Sheet's exact definition was not compared).
- Search looks at the foods, food entries with notes, thoughts and lab test names; reports and values are not searched.
- One tick message now carries the time of the click, so ticking, taking off and ticking again within half an hour does not stop at "log it again?".

2. **Todoist due dates** were only tested against the in-memory fake. Try the first change on a throw-away task.
3. **Sessions from before the deploy** show as "Unknown device" in the Devices list until each device signs in again.
4. **Wide menu:** the top bar now has 12 links and wraps to two lines on a narrow desktop window. Options: move Usage, Labs and Search under a "More" there too, or shorten the names. A tablet in portrait keeps the top bar (the bottom bar starts below 620 px).
5. **Week page vs the Sheet:** I built it from the Sheet's description in the handover, not by comparing it with the Sheet's tab. Which numbers or charts of the Sheet's Weekly summary are missing here (for example saturated fat, sugar, per-meal values)?
6. **Lab upload on the web (16):** photo or PDF, and is it fine that it is read by the AI like in the chat?
7. **Barcode by camera (17):** the phone camera needs the app on HTTPS (it is) and works in the browser; do you want it, or is typing the digits enough?
8. **Linking thoughts (18):** what should a link do (a "related" list on a thought, a tree, a tag)?
9. **A new habit and the Sheet:** the Sheet's Habits tab reads the habit list from the database, so a new or retired habit should show there at the next sync (every 2 minutes); not verified in the Sheet itself.
10. **Search on private entries:** keep them out of the global search (as now), or add a "include private" tick that is only shown on this page?

After deploy 3 the new pages (Habits, Week in four periods, Trends, Search, the four Settings tabs, the usual meals form) were also rendered against

restore a dump only if data was damaged. The new tables are `trusted_devices` and `ui_settings`.
