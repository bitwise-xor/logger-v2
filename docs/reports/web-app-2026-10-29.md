Decisions I took without asking (neutral defaults):
- Undo needs no second confirmation: pressing the button is the yes (a save is also "the yes" elsewhere in the app).
- More shows the open-questions badge on its own tab.
- In a task's edit form on Ideas and tasks, an emptied date field means "unchanged" (removing a date needs the tick box), so a cleared field never deletes a date by accident.
- The signed-out "not found" is a plain 404, not a redirect, so a stranger learns nothing about which pages exist.

## 5. Open questions and things to check

1. **Phone and icon on a real iPhone** are not tested. Checked: the tags and files, and the layout in the desktop browser at 375 px with made-up data (dark mode). Please add the app to the home screen and look at the icon, the bottom bar and the "More" menu, and say what to adjust.
2. **Todoist due dates** were only tested against the in-memory fake. The real call (`due_date`, or `due_string: "no date"` to remove) follows Todoist's API as I know it; try it first on a throw-away task.
3. **Sessions that existed before the deploy** show as "Unknown device" in the list until each device signs in again; nothing else changes for them.
4. **Times on the Devices page** were shown in the server's time zone (UTC) in this deploy; fixed to Berlin time in the next deploy (see the core report).
5. 16 (lab upload on the web: photo or PDF? it would be read by the AI), 17 (camera barcode scanning), 18 (what "link thoughts" should mean): no answers yet.
6. The phone bottom bar replaces the top menu at 620 px and below; a tablet in portrait is above that and keeps the top bar. Say if a tablet should get the bottom bar too.
